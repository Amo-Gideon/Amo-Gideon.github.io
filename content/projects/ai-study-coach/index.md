A multi-agent AI study coach built with AutoGen and Gemini 2.0 Flash that helps plan
study sessions, quizzes the user, and adapts revision schedules to their progress.

<!--more-->

Multiple cooperating AutoGen agents — a planner, a quiz generator, and a coach — converse
with each other and the user, powered by Gemini 2.0 Flash. The system decomposes learning
goals into daily plans, generates practice questions from study material, and revises the
schedule based on quiz performance.
