I am **Gideon Kofi Amo Appau**, a master's student in Software Engineering (AI/ML) at the [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn), advised by [Assoc. Res. Pengkun Wang](http://home.ustc.edu.cn/~pengkun/index.html), and a Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) recipient. I earned my B.Sc. in Information Technology Education (First Class Honours, Top 10%) from the University of Education, Winneba, Ghana.

My research focuses on **continual and long-horizon reinforcement learning** and **learning under data scarcity**. My master's thesis studies class-adaptive sharpness-aware minimization for long-tailed visual recognition: a frozen log-scarcity prior improves tail-class accuracy over Focal-SAM (+2.50 points at imbalance ratio 100, +1.98 at ratio 200, CIFAR-100-LT, 4 seeds each), while the online feedback controller I built to go beyond frequency was falsified under a pre-specified test battery - a rigorous negative result. Alongside the thesis, I maintain a [self-directed RL portfolio](/portfolio/) on world-model planning and control.

**I am seeking funded PhD opportunities for Spring/Fall 2027** in reinforcement learning. Here is my [CV](/cv/) ([PDF](/files/Appau_Gideon_CV.pdf)).
