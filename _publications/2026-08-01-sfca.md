Class frequency is a stable but incomplete prior for optimization difficulty: two equally rare classes can sit in loss regions with very different local sensitivity. SFCA (Sharpness-Feedback Classwise Allocation) tests whether online geometric feedback can improve on frequency, and then subjects that hypothesis to a pre-registered falsification battery. The controller:

* **Ascent-gap observation**: for every class in the mini-batch, measure the change in class-conditioned loss along SAM's own perturbation direction; both losses are already computed by SAM, so no extra forward pass is needed.
* **Robust EMA state**: per-class exponential moving average, median/IQR-centered and clipped, so a tail class seen in only a few difficult batches cannot dominate the allocation.
* **Bounded allocation**: scarcity prior + normalized feedback pass through a sigmoid and are clipped to [0.05, 0.95]; the controller keeps only class-level state and adds no inference overhead.

**Pre-registered falsification battery and verdict** (CIFAR-100-LT, uniform recipe):

* **Polarity null**: positive vs negative feedback weights produce statistically indistinguishable tails, and the sign ordering flips across seeds - under both Logit Adjustment (multiple seeds) and CE (3 seeds).
* **Signal destruction**: shuffling, sign-reversing, or zeroing the feedback is indistinguishable from the intact controller (replicated at a second seed) - the feedback's content is decorative.
* **Channel decomposition**: the scarcity-only controller (feedback removed entirely) matches or beats full SFCA at both principal imbalance ratios; the tail gains come from the frozen prior.
* **Hindsight oracle**: even a privileged allocation computed from the complete training record loses to both the Focal-SAM reference and the frozen scarcity prior (1 seed, directional).
* **Predictive null**: the smoothed ascent gap does not predict future per-class accuracy improvement (Spearman at most 0.04; incremental R-squared at most 0.017), while current accuracy and recent progress predict only mean-reverting noise.
* **Positive control**: the frozen scarcity prior itself improves tail accuracy over Focal-SAM by +2.50 points at IR=100 (4 seeds) and +1.98 points at IR=200 (4 seeds) under the same recipe.

**Conclusion**: on this benchmark family, no class-level signal beyond class frequency was detectable, and online geometric feedback adds nothing measurable on top of it. SFCA's contribution is a boundary result: a complete closed-loop controller, built and then falsified under registered tests. The earlier figure sometimes quoted for this project (tail 22.45% to 24.49% at IR=200) comes from a pre-uniform-recipe comparison of the frozen prior against Focal-SAM; it is superseded by the 4-seed uniform-recipe numbers above. The CIFAR-100-C evaluation (exploratory, pre-uniform-recipe checkpoints) is a robustness screen, not evidence for the feedback channel.
